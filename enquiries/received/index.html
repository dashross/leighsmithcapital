How kind

A note

How kind of you to write. It has gone nowhere. The form is not attached to anything, and was never meant to be.

The partners have kept the same friends since school and find they have quite enough of them. Introductions, when they happen at all, happen at the club, on the water, or after a funeral. Never through a website.

One does not correspond with people one has not been introduced to. We are sure you understand. If you do not, that is rather the point.

Please do not trouble yourself to write again.

Do give our regards to whoever gave you the address

Leigh Smith Capital
