The Firm

This page exists because people expect one

The partners

Two. They have known one another since school. Neither is named here, and neither will be.

Origins

Both families came over to New England in the seventeenth century and have kept their money together since the nineteenth. That is the part of the history which concerns anyone else.

Record

Known to the families, to their accountants, and to the people who were in the room. It is not a long list and it is not looking to be added to.

Temperament

Slow. The Firm has declined a great deal and regretted very little of it. It has never made an investment because a round was closing, and it does not propose to begin.

Elsewhere

The partners’ civic, charitable and sporting commitments are their own. Enquiries about them are not answered.
